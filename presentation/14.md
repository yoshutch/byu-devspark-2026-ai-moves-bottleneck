# Find the Constraint

Look for the place where work waits, piles up, or comes back for rework.

| Signal | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| **Aging queue** | Where are pull requests, findings, approvals, or releases waiting? |
| **Rework** | Where do we repeatedly fix defects, clarify requirements, or undo decisions? |
| **Scarce capacity** | Which people, environments, CI runners, or release windows limit flow? |
| **Slow feedback** | How long until we know whether the change helped users? |

## Match the intervention to the constraint

- **Understanding:** DDD, clear requirements, domain language, and prototypes.
- **Feedback and review:** TDD, CI, small changes, and explicit ownership.
- **Release and operations:** small batches, progressive delivery, observability, and runbooks.
- **Learning:** user feedback, outcome metrics, and continuous improvement.

## AI can still help!
- AI is great at creating throw-away code for fast-prototyping = faster user feedback before expensive development processes
- AI is great at looking through vast amounts of information = quickly research alternatives, quickly debug thousands of logs, etc.
- AI is great at challenging your assumptions = let it find edge cases, let if question your prompts with suggestions on how to improve something


> [!Important]
> You can improve speed and decrease throughput by adding more operating expenses than the amount of value added by the change introduced.
